Yes. Registration is normally $549. For October 10th your seat, a hotel suite when available, lunch and a guest seat are all covered. The only thing it costs you is a Saturday.

Licensed chiropractors who want a more profitable practice, less dependence on insurance, or more disc, neuropathy and knee cases through the door. It works whether you already own decompression equipment or you're weighing it up.

Trump National in Jupiter, Florida. Palm Beach International (PBI) is the closest major airport. Confirm your seat and suite with Lacey before you book your flight.

Yes. Your registration includes one guest seat, based on availability. An associate or your CA is a good choice, because the systems work best when the whole team has heard them.

No. The Chiro Event is a business and clinical training day rather than a CE course. Continuing education is offered separately through our national certifications.
